Source ID: POL-008
Title: Backup and Recovery Standard
Version: 1.3
Effective date: 2025-10-01
Status: current

Tier 1 systems must have a recovery point objective (RPO) of 15 minutes and a recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours.
Backups must be copied to a second AWS Region.
Restore tests must be run every quarter for Tier 1 systems and the results recorded.
