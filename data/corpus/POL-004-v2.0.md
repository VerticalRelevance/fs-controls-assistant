Source ID: POL-004
Title: Logging and Monitoring Standard
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2026-01-15
Status: current

Audit logs for production systems must be retained for 400 days.
Audit logs must be stored in a separate logging account with write-once protection.
Security alerts of high severity must be triaged within 30 minutes during business hours and within 2 hours outside business hours.
Related control: CTL-004.
