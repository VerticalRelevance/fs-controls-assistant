Source ID: POL-005
Title: Third-Party Risk Standard
Version: 1.4
Effective date: 2025-07-01
Status: current

A vendor risk assessment must be completed and approved before a new vendor is onboarded.
Vendors rated high risk must be reassessed every year. Medium risk vendors every two years.
Vendors that process customer data must provide a SOC 2 Type II report or equivalent.
Related control: CTL-005.
