Title: Access Management Policy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2024-06-01
Status: superseded

Status: SUPERSEDED by POL-001 version 2.0 (effective 2026-03-01).

Access to production databases must be reviewed once a year (annual access review) by the system owner.
Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access.
Privileged access may be granted for up to 7 days.
