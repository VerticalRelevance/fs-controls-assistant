Source ID: POL-007
Title: Data Classification Standard
Version: 1.1
Effective date: 2025-05-15
Status: current

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Restricted.
Customer personal information (names, account numbers, government IDs) is Restricted.
Restricted data must not be copied to non-production environments. Synthetic or masked data must be used for testing.
Restricted data must not be shared in chat tools or tickets.
