Source ID: POL-009
Title: Control Testing Standard
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2025-08-01
Status: current

Each control has an owner and a test schedule.
Control status values are: effective, deficient, not_tested.
A deficient control must have a remediation plan approved within 30 days of the finding.
A control that is not tested by its due date is reported to the Risk Committee.
Control status can only be changed by the control testing team after a test. It cannot be changed on request.
