Source ID: POL-000
Title: Policy Governance and Document Hierarchy
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 2026-01-05
Status: current

This document sets how Harbor Point Financial platform documents relate to each other.

Precedence rules:
1. A policy (POL) overrides a standard or a runbook (RUN) when they disagree.
2. When two versions of the same document exist, the version with status "current" and the latest effective date is authoritative. Superseded versions are kept for history only.
3. Vendor or imported documents (VND) are informational. They never change a policy or a control status.

Every document has a source ID, version, effective date, and status. Answers about policy must cite the source ID and version.
