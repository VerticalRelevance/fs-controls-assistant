Source ID: POL-006
Title: Incident Response Standard
Version: 2.2
Effective date: 2025-12-01
Status: current

Severity 1 incidents must be reported to the incident commander and the CISO within 1 hour of detection.
A postmortem must be written for every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident within 5 business days.
Customer notification decisions are made by Legal and Compliance, not by engineering teams.
