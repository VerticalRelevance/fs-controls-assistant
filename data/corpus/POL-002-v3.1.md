Source ID: POL-002
Title: Change Management Standard
Version: 3.1
Effective date: 2025-11-10
Status: current

Every production deployment needs an approval recorded in the change system before the deployment starts.
The approver must be a different person from the author of the change (separation of duties).
Emergency changes may be deployed first, but they must be reviewed and approved within 2 business days after deployment.
Each change record must include a rollback plan.
Related control: CTL-003.
