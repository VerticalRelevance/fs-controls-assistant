Source ID: POL-001
Title: Access Management Policy
Version: 2.0
Effective date: 2026-03-01
Status: current

This version supersedes POL-001 version 1.0.

Access to production databases must be reviewed every quarter (quarterly access review) by the system owner. The review must be completed within 10 business days after the quarter ends.
Multi-factor authentication is required for all access to production systems, not only remote access.
Privileged access is time-bound and expires after 8 hours. Standing administrator access is not allowed.
Accounts of people who leave the company must be disabled within 24 hours.
Related control: CTL-001.
